GitHub

Where your platform lives, and how it is published

Repository

Every file of your platform: app, dashboard, lessons, your views.

Commits

Every change is saved with a note. Nothing is lost; any change can be undone.

Pages

Publishes the repository as your site, a minute after each change.

Your repository is public. Your data is not in it: it lives in Supabase.

Supabase

Where your data lives

Tables

What the app recorded. You meet all four in lesson 1.

SQL Editor

Where you run the SQL your assistant writes: practice data, your views.

Your sign-in

The account your dashboard uses to read the data.

Nobody can change or delete what was recorded: not you, not your assistant. You build on top of it.

Claude

Your AI assistant

  • Works in the cloud, on your repository. Nothing to install.
  • Knows the course: reads the lesson, knows your progress, follows the rules of the platform.
  • Asks before it builds: you define what a number means. It never decides that for you.
  • Writes the views and panels, shows you the SQL to run, and publishes your site.

Cohort and retention

A group, and whether it comes back

A cohort is a group defined by what its members did: everyone whose first order was in week 1.

Retention follows that group: what share ordered again in each later week?

In class · Mixpanel

Find each idea in a real tool

  • Open the demo project. Find an event and its properties.
  • Build a funnel of three steps. Then break it down by one property.
  • Add a filter. Watch the numbers change, and say who is now in them.
  • Look at retention for one cohort.

For every number, ask: who is counted, and who decided?

Lesson 0 · Summary

What you learnt

  • Your assistant builds; you decide what every number means.
  • Events, properties, funnels, breakdowns, filters, cohorts, retention: each one hides a definition.
