One-time setup

About 20 minutes, once, before lesson 1. Three parts: your copy of the platform on GitHub, your database on Supabase, and your AI assistant in Claude. Nothing to install.

Part 1 · Your copy of the platform (GitHub)
1

Sign in to GitHub

At github.com. If you don't have an account, create a free one.

2

Make your own copy (a "fork")

  1. Open the course repository: github.com/asafbarzilay-web/data-mindset-template
  2. Click Fork (top right, circled below), then Create fork.

a repository named your-name/data-mindset-template, with "forked from…" under its name. This is yours: every step from now on happens there.

Working as a group? One of you forks; then Settings → Collaborators → Add people to add the others.

3

Publish your site

  1. Go to your fork, the repository you just created: github.com/your-name/data-mindset-template. Under its name it says "forked from asafbarzilay-web/data-mindset-template". (Not the course's original.)
  2. Click Settings (the last tab along the top), then Pages in the menu on the left.
  3. Under Build and deployment: Source Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root). Click Save.
  4. Wait a minute and refresh the page.

"Your site is live at https://your-name.github.io/data-mindset-template/". Keep that address: you need it in step 9.

If Pages says "Upgrade or make this repository public", you are not on your fork: go back to the first point.

Part 2 · Your database (Supabase)
4

Create a project

  1. At supabase.com, sign in (with your GitHub account is easiest).
  2. First time on Supabase? It asks you to create an organization: name it with your own name, type Personal, plan Free.
  3. Click New project and fill in:
    Project name: data-mindset
    Database password: click Generate a password, and save it in your password manager
    Region: Central EU (Frankfurt)
  4. Click Create new project and wait until it's ready (a minute or two).

the project's home page, with its address https://….supabase.co near the top.

5

Create the tables

  1. Click this button:
  2. In Supabase: SQL Editor → New query. Paste (⌘V), and click Run. If it warns about destructive operations, confirm: the setup only creates what is missing.

a small result table listing sessions, clicks, selections, identities.

6

Create your sign-in for the dashboard

  1. Authentication → Users → Add user → Create new user.
  2. Your email and a password you choose. Keep Auto Confirm User ticked. Click Create user.
7

Let the dashboard read your views

Everything you build in this course is a view, and your views live in their own space in the database, called analysis, apart from the raw data. Out of the box, Supabase only lets a website read the main space (public). This step adds analysis, so your dashboard can show what you build.

  1. Click Project Settings: the gear icon at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  2. In the settings menu, click Data API (in some versions it is called API).
  3. Find the field Exposed schemas. It already lists public and graphql_public.
  4. Click into the field, choose (or type) analysis, and click Save.

analysis listed in Exposed schemas, next to public.

Skip this and nothing breaks today, but from lesson 1 on, every panel you build says it can't find its view.

Part 3 · Your AI assistant (Claude)
8

Start your assistant in the cloud

  1. In the Claude app: Code → + New.
  2. Click Local (above the message box) → Cloud → Add cloud environment…
  3. Name: Data-mindset. Network access: Full (so it can reach your database). Click Add environment.
  4. Connect Claude to your GitHub account (first time only). Click the </> button, next to Data-mindset. If the list is empty or it offers to connect GitHub, follow its prompts: sign in to GitHub and allow Claude to access your repositories, at least data-mindset-template. This is how Claude can see your fork from step 2.
  5. Back at the </> button (press Refresh list if needed), pick your-name/data-mindset-template.

above the message box: Data-mindset · data-mindset-template · main.

The environment is created once. Next time: + New, and it remembers Data-mindset and your repository.

9

Register for the course: your assistant asks, you answer

This step connects your platform and registers you for the course: until it's done, your lecturer can't see you or open lessons for you. Your assistant collects your name and three addresses and puts them in the right place.

  1. In the message box, type: "Register me for the course".
  2. It asks you four things, one at a time, and tells you where to find each:
    · your name, as your lecturer knows you
    · your site's address, from step 3 (https://your-name.github.io/data-mindset-template/)
    · your Supabase project URL: in Supabase, Project Settings → API (starts with https:// and ends with .supabase.co)
    · your publishable key: Project Settings → API Keys, the one starting sb_publishable_
  3. Paste each answer when it asks for it.

it confirms they are saved and that your site is updated (give it a minute).

Never give it, or anyone, the secret key (sb_secret_…). If you paste one by mistake, it will tell you to delete that key in Supabase.

10

Your assistant checks everything and confirms you're registered; you sign in once

Right after step 9, your assistant checks your setup by itself and reports one line per check:

  1. database setup: step 5 ran
  2. analysis is exposed: step 7 is done
  3. capture works: it writes a test visit and a tap exactly as the app does, and finds them in your database

ALL GOOD, then "Reported to your lecturer: setup done." You're registered. If a line says FAIL, it tells you which step to redo.

Then open your dashboard (it gives you the link) and sign in with the email and password from step 6.

your dashboard, with a board for each lesson.

11

Meet your assistant

  1. Back in the Claude app, in the same session, type: "What are we working on?"

it says you're on lesson 0, Tools and concepts, and gives you the link to its slides.

If it talks about something else, you're probably not in your repository: check step 8.

That's it. From now on, every lesson starts from your home page, and your assistant starts from + New in the Claude app.
